**Meridian Landfall 1.0** for [Beyond All Reason](https://www.beyondallreason.info/): a 32 × 36 map for 8v8.

Starship Meridian has come down on an asteroid. Port fights starboard inside the ship, as on Starship Meridian, and outside on the cratered moonrock round it.

- **The ship is Starship Meridian's own**, in the middle of the map. Its four start rooms a side, its five crossings through the bridge, command room, reactor, shield generator and engine room, and its metal are all as on that map. The hangars' outboard walls now open in bay doors onto the moonrock.
- **Sixteen starts.** Port is 0 to 7: four in the ship, and four outside on the flank and the fields at the bow and the stern, each on a dark, level mare. Starboard's 8 to 15 are their mirror images. The two halves match exactly, rock for rock.
- **Outside,** the moonrock lies round the ship at its deck's height, with two more ways across: round the bow and round the stern. Each team's bay doors join its outside players to its hangars.
- **Craters:**
  - **Small craters and bowls:** about 65 a side. Their walls are too steep for vehicles and hovers and gentle enough for bots, with no ramps in or out. The biggest hold rich metal or a geothermal vent on their floors. Amphibious tanks climb like bots, so they get in too.
  - **Void pits:** four a side, blasted right through the asteroid, each into a different rock with its own ore: iron with rust-red seams, copper with green seams, ilmenite with silvery seams that glint, and pale anorthosite with gold-yellow seams. Their layered, stepped walls drop into the stars. Their rims are ridged and cracked, and strewn with stones, slabs, shards and flecks of ore. Only aircraft cross them.
  - **The asteroid's edge** is ragged at every scale. It is worn down into a long slope, as if water had run off it for years, gullied and rilled, falling away into the void.
- **No circles but the ship's:** every crater wavers, and every pit and the edge are broken where rock blew out.
- **Metal:**
  - the ship's;
  - three spots at each outside start and four more on each flank;
  - four contested in the fields at the bow and the stern on each side;
  - rich metal on the floors of two bowls a side (bots only).
  - Each team has 72 metal/s of its own, 69 is contested, and there are 7 vents.
- **No wind and no tide.**
- **Everything was modelled and painted for this map or for Starship Meridian:**
  - the ship and all its craft and machines;
  - moonrock boulders, broken slabs and shards;
  - the ship's landing struts;
  - cargo set down at the bay doors;
  - the landing burn's soot and the engines' blast marks on the regolith.
- The minimap and lobby picture are the map seen straight down in 3D, with the starfield showing through the void.
- The gadgets are from All That Simmers by Nikuksis.

**Install:** put `meridian_landfall_1.0.sd7` in BAR's `maps` folder (usually `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Beyond-All-Reason\data\maps`). To see its picture on the lobby's setup screen, put `Meridian_Landfall_1.0.jpg` in `data\LuaMenu\Images\Minimaps` and restart the lobby.

**Not yet tried in a game.** Invisible, indestructible rubble covers the void's floor so that no ground unit, not even a spider, can cross it. Each blocker's collision box is buried far under the ground so that shots pass. That is new and worth a test. The map has no start boxes.
