**Marram Line 1.0** for [Beyond All Reason](https://www.beyondallreason.info/): a 24 × 24 map for 4v4.

A coast where an old war dug in and never left. Two trench lines face each other across a dead man's zone, one dug into the last of the dunes, the other at the foot of a gentle hill of hedgerowed fields. A tidal inlet carries ships in from the sea, through both lines, to a sandy cove.

- **From west to east:** the sea, a wide beach with its old obstacles, a foredune cut by three draws, a belt of dunes, the beach team's trenches, the dead man's zone, the hill team's trenches, then a slight rise to a crest with a ruined village.
- **The beach team** (starts 0 to 3) has its bases in the dunes' flat slacks and on a rocky headland, with the open sea behind it.
  - **The beach:** steel hedgehogs, stakes and wrecked landing craft. Vehicles get off it only by the three draws, the headland's road and the inlet's bank; bots climb the foredune anywhere.
  - **The dunes:** 86 of them, with gentle backs and steep slip faces that only bots climb, marram on them and pines behind.
  - **The headland:** cliffs only spiders climb, a lighthouse and an old coastal gun's casemate on its point.
- **The hill team** (starts 4 to 7) has its bases on the rise among fields and hedgerows, with a ruined village, its church and an orchard on the crest.
  - **The cove:** the inlet's end, behind its line, a sheltered harbour ringed with sand, with a jetty and boat sheds.
  - **The sandy end:** its own stretch of open coast south of the inlet, reached by land only from the hill.
- **The trenches:** each line is a crenellated fire trench with a fire step, a support trench behind it and communication trenches back to the bases, with machine-gun nests and two belts of wire in front.
  - **Bots** climb in and out anywhere. **Vehicles and hovers** never get in: they cross at four gaps a line, each covered by a concrete bunker.
  - **The wire and the hedges** are crushed by tanks but stop bots and hovers, so tanks open the way for the infantry.
  - **Each line bulges** into the zone once, on opposite flanks, round a dune tongue and round a knoll with a windmill.
- **The dead man's zone:** a shelled meadow between the lines, 1,400 to 2,200 across. Bomb craters only bots get into, shell holes, wrecked tanks to reclaim, shattered trees and a ruined farm with a geothermal vent. Eight rich metal spots.
- **The inlet:** 24 to 32 deep from the sea to the cove, so every ship, battleships included, sails up it into the fight. No land unit crosses it. Each line ends short of it, with dragon's teeth running into the water and a bunker covering it.
- **Not symmetric:** the beach team's open sea against the hill team's height, cove and sandy end. The map was checked for fair shares: each start's driving distance to the zone matches its opposite number's within 8%, each team's metal is the same, and their reclaim and level ground to build on are within 3% and 9%.
- **Metal:** four spots at each start (2.0), five more on each side's own ground (2.4), one in each salient (2.8) and eight in the zone (3.0): 46.8 metal/s a team and 24.0 in the zone.
- **Vents:** one for each team and one in the zone. Wind 6 to 18, tidal 18.
- **Everything was modelled and painted for this map:** hedgehogs, landing craft, bunkers, the casemate, the lighthouse, dragon's teeth, sandbag nests, wire, wrecked tanks and field guns, the village, the church, the farm and the windmill, hedgerow bushes and oaks, pines, apple trees, hay ricks, marram and reeds, the jetty and boat sheds. No national markings anywhere.
- The minimap and lobby picture are the map seen straight down in 3D.
- The detail textures and gadgets are from All That Simmers by Nikuksis.

**Install:** put `marram_line_1.0.sd7` in BAR's `maps` folder (usually `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Beyond-All-Reason\data\maps`). To see its picture on the lobby's setup screen, put `Marram_Line_1.0.jpg` in `data\LuaMenu\Images\Minimaps` and restart the lobby.

**Not yet tried in a game.** The balance between the two sides is a first guess, and the map has no start boxes: play with the fixed starts (0 to 3 the beach team, 4 to 7 the hill team).
