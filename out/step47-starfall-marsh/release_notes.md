**Starfall Marsh 1.0** for [Beyond All Reason](https://www.beyondallreason.info/): a 16 × 16 map for 2v2.

Centuries ago a giant warship crashed into a jungle marsh. It ploughed a furrow, broke its back, and buried its nose in the dirt it pushed up. The jungle has been taking it back ever since.

- **The ship** lies across the middle, in angular, layered armour of gunmetal, faded crimson and brass, with cyan seams still faintly alight.
  - Its engines stand over a reed marsh in the flooded furrow.
  - Its aft hull carries the bridge tower and the ship's crest.
  - It broke in two. The torn ends spill decks, ribs, cables and a glowing reactor.
  - The fore hull dives nose-first into a huge mound of overgrown dirt. Only its prow blade and broken spires show on the mound's crest.
  - The hull is moss-covered, vine-hung and sprouting ferns and saplings. It is indestructible and walls the two halves apart.
- **Three ways across:**
  - the marsh past the engines (shallow, wadeable);
  - the breach where the ship broke;
  - over the mound or round it. A graded track climbs its north-east flank, and its west face is for bots only.
- **Wreckage** lies everywhere: ribs, girders, engine bells and pods, armour plates and slabs, fins, spires, reactor cores and hatches with the crest. It is heaviest at the crash site, with only small scraps out by the bases. It holds about 7,200 metal a team to reclaim.
- **Metal:**
  - four spots at each start (2.0 each);
  - six on the wreck (2.8 each): by the engines, at the breach and on the mound's crest, on each side.
- **Geothermal vents:** two, where the reactor spilled at the break.
- **The jungle:** kapok and banyan giants, oaks, palms by the water, mangroves and reeds in the shallows, and ferns everywhere. A humid haze hangs over it. Wind is 4 to 12; tidal is 6.
- The south half mirrors the north. Teams 0 and 1 start north (west, east), 2 and 3 south. The ship, its wreckage and the reeds were modelled and painted for this map. The trees, ferns, detail textures and gadgets are from All That Simmers by Nikuksis.

**Install:** put `starfall_marsh_1.0.sd7` in BAR's `maps` folder (usually `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Beyond-All-Reason\data\maps`). To see its picture on the lobby's setup screen, put `Starfall_Marsh_1.0.jpg` in `data\LuaMenu\Images\Minimaps` and restart the lobby.

**Not yet tried in a game.** The reclaim amount is a first guess, and the map has no start boxes.
