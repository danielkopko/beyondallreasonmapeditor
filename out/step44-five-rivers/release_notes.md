**Five Rivers 1.0** for [Beyond All Reason](https://www.beyondallreason.info/): a 28 × 28 map for five kings and their peasants, 20 players (5 teams of 4).

- **Each king** holds an oval cliff-top hall on the rim. A graded ramp faces the king's peasants; bots-only zigzag switchbacks climb the other three sides. The cliffs stop everything but spiders.
- **Five rivers** run out from a lake between the realms. A stone bridge and a gravel ford cross each one, and a bridge from each realm crosses the lake to the isle.
- **Metal:** 75 spots, 24.3 metal/s a team (peasants 1.8, kings 2.0, fords 3.0, the isle 3.4), and 11 geothermal vents: one on each hall, one in each realm, one on the isle.
- **The Bloom planet:** a glowing night world of teal moss, cyan-lit rivers, giant trees, mushrooms and crystals. Wind 3 to 12, tidal 20.
- Every fifth of the map is the same, turned about the centre. Plants, detail textures and gadgets are from All That Simmers by Nikuksis.

**Install:** put `five_rivers_1.0.sd7` in BAR's `maps` folder (usually `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Beyond-All-Reason\data\maps`). For its picture on the lobby's setup screen, put `Five_Rivers_1.0.jpg` in `data\LuaMenu\Images\Minimaps` and restart the lobby.

Not yet tried in a game: worth checking that the night isn't too dark, that extractors go down on the ford spots (10 deep), and that your lobby takes 20 players. There are no start boxes in the map; the fixed starts go A's king, A's three peasants, then B, C, D and E clockwise.

**Rebuilt 2026-10-23:** the trees, ferns, mushrooms, crystals and rocks now face every way, as they were meant to. In the first upload of this version they all faced south. If you downloaded it before then, replace your copy.
