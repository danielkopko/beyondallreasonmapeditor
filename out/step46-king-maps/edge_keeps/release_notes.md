**Edge Keeps 1.0** for [Beyond All Reason](https://www.beyondallreason.info/): a 24 × 24 map for four kings and their peasants, 16 players (4 teams of 4).

- **Each king holds a keep**, a granite cliff-top jutting from the middle of an edge, 120 above the moor.
- **A ramp** climbs each keep from the loch side, for every unit. **Bots-only zigzags** climb both of its flanks. Vehicles and hovercraft can't take them.
- **The peasants** hold the corner beside their keep, going clockwise, under the next king's cliff, so two kings look down on every corner. Granite crags stand between the corners and the loch.
- **A loch** lies in the middle, 60 deep, with an isle. Stone causeways cross to the isle from each keep's road.
- **Metal:** 56 spots, 28.4 metal/s a team:
  - nine for the peasants (1.8 each);
  - two on each keep (2.0);
  - two on the moor (2.2 and 2.6);
  - one on the isle (3.4).
- **Geothermal vents:** 9, one on each keep, one in each corner and one on the isle.
- **The Heath planet:** a highland moor of rolling heather and moor grass, with peat hags and green flushes in the hollows and bracken on the slopes. Pinewoods and birch copses grow by the loch, with granite tors on the crests and cairns on the keeps' rims. The sky is grey with drifting mist. Wind is 10 to 25; tidal is 12.
- Every quarter of the map is the same, turned about the centre. Detail textures and gadgets are from All That Simmers by Nikuksis. The firs and birches are from Crimson Bay.

**Install:** put `edge_keeps_1.0.sd7` in BAR's `maps` folder (usually `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Beyond-All-Reason\data\maps`). To see its picture on the lobby's setup screen, put `Edge_Keeps_1.0.jpg` in `data\LuaMenu\Images\Minimaps` and restart the lobby.

**Not yet tried in a game.** The map has no start boxes. The fixed starts go A's king, then A's three peasants, then B, C and D clockwise, with A north.

**Rebuilt 2026-10-23:** the trees, bracken, rocks and cairns now face every way, as they were meant to. In the first upload of this version they all faced south. If you downloaded it before then, replace your copy.
