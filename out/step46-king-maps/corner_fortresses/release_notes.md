**Corner Fortresses 1.0** for [Beyond All Reason](https://www.beyondallreason.info/): a 24 × 24 map for four kings and their peasants, 16 players (4 teams of 4).

- **Each king holds a corner fortress**: a raised, paved yard behind earth ramparts, with a tower either side of its gate.
- **A ramp** climbs through the gate, facing the middle, for every unit. **Bots-only zigzags** cut through both inner ramparts. Vehicles and hovercraft can't take them. There are no doors: the ramparts are earth, flat on top, so you can build on them.
- **All twelve peasants** live in the middle, round a brick market square, each team's three nearest its king. **Acid lakes** part the fortresses at the middle of each edge, and they hurt anything in them.
- **Metal:** 64 spots, 32.8 metal/s a team:
  - nine for the peasants (1.8 each);
  - three in each fortress (2.0);
  - two in the fields (2.2);
  - one between two teams' fields (2.8);
  - one in the market (3.4).
- **Reclaim:** about 9,100 metal a team lies in the wrecks and rubble.
- **Geothermal vents:** 9, one in each fortress, one in each team's fields and one in the market.
- **The Forge planet:** a derelict industrial estate. Lots of cracked, broken concrete lie between gravel waste ground gone to weeds, with rust and oil stains and worn asphalt roads. Ruined works stand among them: wrecked factories with their storage tanks and metal makers, and rubble. There are container yards, trucks by the roads, and a crane truck by the market. The sky is brown smog with yellow haze. Wind is 4 to 14; tidal is 10.
- Every quarter of the map is the same, turned about the centre. Detail textures and gadgets are from All That Simmers by Nikuksis. The crates and trucks are from Industrial Revolution.

**Install:** put `corner_fortresses_1.0.sd7` in BAR's `maps` folder (usually `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Beyond-All-Reason\data\maps`). To see its picture on the lobby's setup screen, put `Corner_Fortresses_1.0.jpg` in `data\LuaMenu\Images\Minimaps` and restart the lobby.

**Not yet tried in a game.** The acid's damage (`water.damage` 60) and the amount of reclaim are first guesses. The map has no start boxes. The fixed starts go A's king, then A's three peasants, then B, C and D clockwise.

**Rebuilt 2026-10-23:** the wrecks, rubble, crates and trucks (parked along the roads) now face every way, as they were meant to. In the first upload of this version they all faced south. If you downloaded it before then, replace your copy.
