**Star Fort 1.0** for [Beyond All Reason](https://www.beyondallreason.info/): a 28 × 28 map for five kings and their peasants, 20 players (5 teams of 4).

- **One star fort holds all five kings.** Each king has a raised bastion, a flagstone court behind earth ramparts, with a tower at each corner. Round the spire in the middle, the five bastions make the star's points.
- **A ramp** climbs in at each bastion's point, between two towers, facing its peasants. Everything climbs it.
- **Bots-only zigzags** cut through the ramparts up both flanks of each bastion. Vehicles and hovercraft can't climb them. There are no doors: the ramparts are earth, flat on top, so you can build on them.
- **The peasants** hold the five realms of dunes outside the fort. Sandstone ridges part the realms, with a bots-only pass over each, and mountains ring the edge.
- **Metal:** 70 spots, 27.2 metal/s a team:
  - nine for the peasants (1.8 each);
  - two in each bastion (2.0);
  - two out by the mountains (2.2);
  - one at each ridge's foot, contested by two realms (2.6).
- **Geothermal vents:** 10, one in each bastion and one out in each realm.
- **The Dune planet:** golden dunes and wind-polished sandstone under a deep blue sky with sand haze. There are palm and fern oases at each ramp's foot, desert rocks on the ridges, scree under the cliffs, and old solar and wind wrecks half-buried in the sand. Wind is 12 to 28; tidal is 0.
- Every fifth of the map is the same, turned about the centre. Palms, detail textures and gadgets are from All That Simmers by Nikuksis.

**Install:** put `star_fort_1.0.sd7` in BAR's `maps` folder (usually `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Beyond-All-Reason\data\maps`). To see its picture on the lobby's setup screen, put `Star_Fort_1.0.jpg` in `data\LuaMenu\Images\Minimaps` and restart the lobby.

**Not yet tried in a game.** Check that your lobby takes 20 players. The map has no start boxes. The fixed starts go A's king, then A's three peasants, then B, C, D and E clockwise, with A north.

**Rebuilt 2026-10-23:** the palms, ferns, rocks and wrecks now face every way, as they were meant to. In the first upload of this version they all faced south. If you downloaded it before then, replace your copy.
