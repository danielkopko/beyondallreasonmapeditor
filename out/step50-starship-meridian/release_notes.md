**Starship Meridian 1.0** for [Beyond All Reason](https://www.beyondallreason.info/): a 24 × 28 map for 4v4.

A battle-carrier adrift in space, boarded from both sides at once. Port fights starboard for the ship's centreline: the bridge, the command room, the reactor, the shield generator and the engine room.

- **The ship fills the map,** its bow north and its engines at the stern. Around it is space: the engine draws a starfield there instead of ground. The hull's rim walls it off from every ground unit but spiders, and 98% of it is too steep to build on.
- **The two halves are mirror images** across the centreline, rooms, metal and features alike. Port trim is red and starboard green, a ship's navigation colours.
- **Each team's half** is two columns of rooms on one deck, walled by bulkheads, beside its own passage that runs the length of the ship:
  - **four start rooms:** the forward and main hangars outboard (fighters, a dropship and a cargo shuttle on their landing circles), and the crew deck and cargo hold inboard, facing the centreline;
  - **five more rooms:** the weapons bay, the power room (with the team's own geothermal vent), the armory, the thruster bay and the machine shop.
- **The centreline** is shared, and each room on it is a way across:
  - **the bridge,** raised high at the bow, with a ramp up from each side's passage, consoles before a long viewport, and a grand stair down into the command room that only bots take;
  - **the command room (CIC),** round a glowing holo-table;
  - **the main reactor,** its core glowing in a sunken well, with a ramp down from each side's door and two geothermal vents on the well's floor;
  - **the shield generator,** its emitter on three pylons;
  - **the engine room,** the drive core on its grating, with coolant towers aft.
  - Wide spine doors join the shared rooms bow to stern, so a team holding one can push along the centreline.
- **Walls are terrain at about 70°.** No vehicle, bot or hover climbs them, and they stop direct fire. All-terrain spiders climb them, so they can flank over the bulkheads. Every door is at least 500 wide, and the crossing doors 700.
- **Metal:**
  - three spots round each start (2.0 each);
  - one each in the weapons bay, armory, thruster bay and machine shop, and two in the power room (2.4);
  - two in each shared room (3.0; 3.4 in the reactor).
  - Each team has 38.4 metal/s of its own, and 30.8 is shared. There are 4 geothermal vents.
- **No wind in space** and no tide, so energy comes from solar, geothermal, fusion and the reclaim.
- **Everything was modelled and painted for this map:** dropships, fighters, cargo shuttles, tow tugs, fuel carts and service towers in the hangars; consoles, the command chair, the holo-table, the viewport and a sensor dome; the reactor's core, capacitor banks, transformers and pipe runs; the shield emitter; the drive core, coolant towers, fuel tanks and six main engines over space; bunks, mess tables, lockers, med pods and hydroponics planters; weapon racks, munitions and missile carts; containers, pallets, crates and a loader; a repair gantry and tool benches; point-defence turrets, an antenna and comm dishes on the hull. The craft, machines and props can be reclaimed: 4,460 metal in each team's rooms and 930 in the shared ones. The landmarks can't.
- The minimap and lobby picture are the ship seen straight down in 3D, its features and heights included, on the starfield.
- The gadgets are from All That Simmers by Nikuksis.

**Install:** put `starship_meridian_1.0.sd7` in BAR's `maps` folder (usually `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Beyond-All-Reason\data\maps`). To see its picture on the lobby's setup screen, put `Starship_Meridian_1.0.jpg` in `data\LuaMenu\Images\Minimaps` and restart the lobby.

**Not yet tried in a game.** It is the first of these maps whose space isn't drawn (`voidGround`): a transport could drop units onto the invisible ground there, where they would stand stuck. The map has no start boxes.
