# Starship Meridian 1.1: checklist

`starship_meridian_1.1.sd7` ("Starship Meridian 1.1", short name `StarshipMeridian`, map id 20261124) is a 24 × 28 map for 8v8 (1.0 was 4v4).

**1.1 makes Meridian a metal map, opens up the middle, adds thruster platforms and an outer walk, strips the ship bare, and fixes the walls beside the bridge ramps.**

- **A metal map:** the whole ship reads as metal, so in BAR it has to be extractable everywhere, as on BAR's metal maps. Every deck floor now holds metal, with Cloud9_V2's values (see Metal below), and 1.0's 46 spots and their round hatches are gone. This changes the economy completely from 1.0.
- **The middle opened up:** each passage is 1,000 wide (it was 600), the inboard rooms 400 narrower; the crossing doors into the reactor, shield generator and engine room are 1,150 to 1,500 wide (they were 700; the CIC's stay 700, below the bridge ramps), and the spine doors between the shared rooms 1,400 (they were 688). Fewer walls in the middle, for more kinds of unit; the back line's walls stay, cover from long-range guns.
- **Thruster platforms:** each of the three engine housings at the stern carries a platform at 440, 100 over the bulkheads and open (no wall over it, so guns fire over), with the outer walk round it. Its ramp rises out of the room below to a flat step at the outer walk's height, then on up to the platform; from the step's sides a narrow path skirts round the platform's front corners to the outer walk: the outboard ones in each side's thruster bay, the centre one in the engine room. The main engines hang below the outer walk at the housings' aft edges, wholly under its surface (their highest point 10 below it) and clear of the rough ground of space under them (by 33 at the least): at your call their mounting plates, which showed as dark patches on the walk behind the platforms, and the block behind each are gone, and the cones are smaller and raised out of the ground of space, which cut through them. Only the cones remain, coming out of the housings' aft faces. With the bridge at the bow, they give the top and bottom of the map high ground for long-range guns: every part of the deck is within a Starfall's range (6,100) of them or the bridge, by straight line.
- **The outer walk:** a narrow path round the whole hull's edge at 380 (the bridge's height, 40 over the bulkheads, 60 under the platforms), and the bow's upper front ahead of the bridge. Units get onto it from the thruster steps and from the bridge, by a front opening where the viewport was. It looks down on every room, the enemy's back line too, but has no way down into them: off it, the only ways down are the steps and the bridge. It's a sixth way across: up your thruster ramp to its step, round the hull, and down the enemy's.
- **The bridge's walls are a lip,** 30 over its deck (they were 60), low enough for guns there to fire over and still steeper than bots climb.
- **Bare decks:** every feature is gone but the reactor's core, the drive core and the six main engines (energy crystals take their place: below). The reactor's core gives 3,000 energy and the drive core 250, with 50 metal each; the engines are part of the ship.
- **The walls:** on 1.0 the walls there stopped at the bulkheads' 340 while the ramps climb to 380, so near the bridge a ramp ran level with or above the wall tops beside it. Vehicles and bots could drive off the ramp onto the walls, and from there over the bulkhead tops across about 50,000 squares of the ship. Now every wall within 48 of a floor above 280 (the bridge, its landings and the upper bridge ramps) stands at least 30 over that floor, steeper than bots climb, and a new check proves no vehicle, bot, hover or amphibious unit gets onto a wall or the hull's solid tops.

It is one whole starship, a battle-carrier adrift in space, boarded from port and starboard at once. The two fleets fight for the ship's centreline: the bridge, the command room, the reactor, the shield generator and the engine room.

- **Made by the converter here** (`converter/README.md`): the terrain, texture, detail maps and starfield sky, and the features (the reactor's core, the drive core, the main engines), modelled and painted on one atlas. The converter models 35 more (craft, machines, props) that 1.1 leaves out of the map.
- **From All That Simmers v1.1.1:** the featureplacer gadget. Its detail textures aren't used (a ship has no ground for them).
- **The plan** is `DESIGN.md`, with its to-scale drawing (`design/layout.svg`, from `design/layout.py`, whose numbers are `converter/Layout.cs`).

The map is **symmetric**: the starboard half is the port half's mirror image across the ship's centreline (x = 6144), heights and features alike (a check).

**Not tried in BAR yet.** BAR isn't installed on this machine. Everything below was checked with MapCore's copy of the engine's rules, and the looks with MapTool's renderer, not in the game.

**Download:** one file, [starship_meridian_1.1.sd7](https://github.com/danielkopko/BarMaps/releases/download/starship-meridian-1.1/starship_meridian_1.1.sd7), from the public BarMaps release [starship-meridian-1.1](https://github.com/danielkopko/BarMaps/releases/tag/starship-meridian-1.1), with its lobby picture `Starship_Meridian_1.1.jpg`. ## The features

556 placed: 8 of the map's own 3 models, the reactor's core in its well and the drive core in the engine room, on the centreline, and the six main engines, two under each engine housing's aft edge. Energy crystals (1.1, at your call), 548 of them, BAR's own (`pilha_crystal`: the game defines them, so the map carries no model; small 100 energy, medium 200, large 500, a twentieth of that in metal), in clusters of one colour as BAR's own maps lay them (measured on Crystallized Plains: 183 crystals in 22 clusters, nearly all of 8, half small and half medium, 1,200 energy, the crystals about 50 apart; Theta Crystals: clusters of about 19, 3,100 energy): quartz in each team's half, teal in the shared rooms. Each start's circle is edged by two clusters, one on each wall side, in a band just outside its outer ring: 8 crystals and 1,200 energy each, 2,400 a start; the tech start's two are 5 crystals and 600 each, 1,200 (its geothermal vent makes up the rest); the thruster bay's aft start, hard by the hull with the ramp inboard of it, has its two at its fore- and aft-outboard quarters. Each inboard room (no starts) holds two of 12, one against each long wall, diagonally across the room off its four doors: 3,600, half again a start's. Each shared room holds two of 14 with a large crystal in the middle, one each side: 4,800, double a start's: the bridge, the CIC, the shield generator and the engine room. None in the reactor: its core is 3,000 and its geothermal vents are contested. 56 clusters of 5 to 14; each team's rooms hold 32,400 energy in crystals, the shared rooms 19,200: 84,000 in all, about 5,250 a player (Crystallized Plains has 28,100 on a quarter of Meridian's area, Theta Crystals 51,900 on under a third). The clusters are laid by a fixed sequence (`Place.CrystalsPort`), each crystal at least 40 from every other and within 90 of one already in its cluster, at least 80 from any cell's edge, 150 from a door, 120 from a vent and 40 outside any start's circle. The converter still models the other 35 (`Meridian gallery`); 1.1 leaves them out of the map.

## Checked

`check.txt` is the checks on the built map's own heights, round every blocking feature's footprint (16-unit cells turned to the feature's facing, and 8 more round it). 57 pass:
- **The halves:** the heights match their mirror image at every vertex (0.000 apart), and every feature has its mirror image: the same model at the mirrored place and heading.
- **Getting about:** vehicles reach all 16 starts and every vent (the reactor's by the well's ramps); bots reach every start; vehicles reach all three thruster platforms by their ramps, each at 440, 100 over the bulkheads.
- **The outer walk (1.1):** vehicles reach it all round, over the bow, along both sides and across the stern; and from it and the steps' paths (their long edges over the thruster bays, the machine shops and the engine room included), with only the ramps and the bridge's front opening shut, no vehicle, bot, hover or amphibious unit gets down into a room or onto a platform; and each of the three steps' own paths takes vehicles from its landing to the walk beside it, the other ways onto the walk shut.
- **The crossings:** each of the five alone (the outer walk shut too) lets vehicles across; with the five shut, the outer walk alone does; with all six shut, vehicles, bots, hovers and amphibious units can't cross. Spiders still can, over the bulkheads; the check logs it.
- **Space:** no vehicle, bot, hover or amphibious unit reaches it, and 98.3% of it is too steep to build on or drive over.
- **Walls (new in 1.1):** no vehicle, bot, hover or amphibious unit gets onto a wall or the hull's solid tops: no square they reach is off the floors and more than 30 above the highest floor within 24. Spiders get onto 133,868 squares of them; the check logs it. On 1.0 this check fails: vehicles reach 50,150 such squares and bots 50,266, by the bridge ramps.
- **Doors and rooms:** every door is at least 500 wide, and none has a feature in it. Each start room keeps 97% to 98% of its floor level and free to build on (3.1 to 4.5 M square units).
- **Crystals (1.1):** vehicles reach all 548, none is in the reactor, none stands in a doorway, and each side's rooms hold the same energy (32,400; the shared rooms 19,200). They lie in 56 clusters of 5 to 14, none alone, no two crystals closer than 40. Each start's circle is edged by two clusters of 1,200 (the tech start's 600) with none inside its ring; each inboard room holds 3,600 (1.5 times a start's), each shared room but the reactor 4,800 (2 times): the ratios are in the check, so an edit can't drift from them.
- **Standing true:** every feature but the engines stands on ground that rises no more than 4 under its footprint (all 0.0); the six main engines stand at the outer walk's level and wholly under it, nothing of them above its surface, and their cones clear the ground of space under them (1.1).
- **Gun sites (1.1):** no wall within 48 of a thruster platform, step or thruster ramp stands over it, and the bridge's walls stand no more than its 30 lip over its deck. Logged, not a check: what a Starfall (range 6,100) there reaches.
- **Metal (1.1, read back from the built map's own metal map and mapinfo):** raw 79 on every deck floor cell and nowhere else (441,208 cells, 112.9 M square units: 39.2 M in each team's rooms, 26.4 M shared, 8.1 M in doorways and on the outer walk); the metal is its own mirror image, and each team's rooms hold the same; its widest stretch is 13,600 across, far over the 144 (6 × the extractor radius) past which BAR reads a map as a metal map, not spots; a T1 extractor on open deck at every start takes Cloud9's 5.33 metal/s (centred on a cell); and all of it is in reach: vehicles reach 99.8% of the metal cells, bots another 0.1% (the grand stair), and 0.1% is under features. Every vent and start is level. (`metal.txt`, MapTool's spot list, shows the whole deck as one spot with a figure measured at its bounding-box centre, which means nothing for a metal map.)

## Made better on the way

1. **The renderer and `voidGround`** (MapCore, with tests): MapTool's 3D renderer now draws the sky where a `voidGround` map's texture is transparent, as the engine does. A texture update can now keep alpha (DXT1's transparent texels), as space needs; by default it still stores pixels opaque.
2. **The reactor's vents:** at first vehicles couldn't reach them, because the crossing doors' floor was forced to the deck's height over the well's ramps. Doors in the reactor now follow the ramp.
3. **The models' symmetry:** a voxel test of each model's surface against its mirror image (comparing vertices failed symmetric models triangulated the other way). It found real lopsidedness, all fixed: a wing's depth, the blobs' lumps, the weapon rack's loop and the shield emitter's pylons. Only the pallet keeps a twin.
4. **A crater in the bridge's forward wall:** the walls near the centreline rose from the starboard floors before those floors' heights were mirrored in, as if from height 0. The wall dropped to 20 at the centreline and swallowed the viewport. The floors' heights are mirrored first now; no dip is left anywhere on the centreline.
5. **The hull's plating** was a noisy grey checker of 64-unit squares. It's now staggered 256 × 128 plates with seams, lit lips and rivets, and here and there an access hatch or a vent grille.
6. **The decks' stains** were dark blotches everywhere. Now they're fewer and lighter.
7. **The engine room's grating** read as stairs. It's now a framed grid over the drive.
8. **The walls beside the bridge ramps (1.1):** the walls rose from the nearest floor at 70° but stopped at their cap, and beside the ramps that cap is the bulkheads' 340, while the ramps' upper parts and the landings climb to 380. Vehicles and bots drove off the ramps onto the wall tops and then over the hull. Found on Meridian Landfall (since shelved), which builds this ship; walls near floors above 280 now stand at least 30 over them (a lip, steeper than bots climb), and the walls check above guards it.
9. **A metal map (1.1, at your call):** the ship reads as metal at a glance, which in BAR means extractable everywhere, so a spot map would mislead players and wouldn't be accepted, and repainting the decks as some other surface wouldn't change that reading. Every deck floor holds metal now, with the values of Cloud9_V2, a certified BAR metal map; the spots and hatches are gone, and six metal checks replace the spot checks.
10. **Reclaim and bare decks (1.1, at your call):** first every feature but the main engines was made reclaimable, for low metal and some energy by its size (50 to 250 energy, metal a fifth, the reactor's core 3,000 energy); then every feature but the reactor's core, the drive core and the engines was taken off the map, to make room for energy crystals.
11. **The middle and the stern (1.1, at your call):** the centre was mostly walls, which favours a narrow mix of units. The passages are 1,000 wide now and the central doorways wider (but the CIC's, below the bridge ramps); the back line's walls stay. The engine housings, solid in 1.0, carry thruster platforms at 440, open (no wall over them) and reached by ramps with a step part way up; the engines hang below them; the bridge's walls are a 30 lip. Checks prove vehicles reach all three platforms, nothing stands over them, and the bridge's walls stand no higher than the lip.
12. **The outer walk (1.1, at your call):** a path round the hull's edge at the bridge's height, reached from the thruster steps and the bridge's front opening, high ground over every room with no way down into them. Its first cut left a 32-wide trench between the walk and the steps (the walk stopped 96 in, the steps start 128 in), so it didn't join them; it now widens to meet them. Then, at your sketch, the steps became a landing on each ramp with narrow paths from its sides round the platform's corners to the walk, in place of a full-width step across the room's end. Checks prove vehicles reach it all round, each step's path reaches it, it leads down into no room, and it's a sixth way across.

## The minimap and lobby picture

The map's minimap is the ship seen straight down in 3D, with heights, light and features, on the starfield. That minimap is what shows in game, and `MapTool install` draws the lobby's picture from it.
1. `MapTool render3d <map> top.png --size 8192x8192 --camera 6144,7168,0,89.9,72827 --models`. The camera is 5.08 map-lengths up (the 28), as for the other maps, so the decks, the bridge and the hull sit at most a pixel or two out of place.
2. `Meridian minimap top.png minimap.png 3262 3123 4930 5070 1024` cuts the map out of the render and scales it to 1024 square. The crop is the map's corners at the deck's height, projected through the camera: the map is 24 × 28, so it's scaled unevenly, and with space undrawn the background can't show where the map ends. Wherever the render shows its background (space), it puts the starfield.
3. `Meridian setminimap <extracted map> minimap.png` puts it into the SMF.
4. `MapTool pack` packs the map again.

The minimap is `minimap_3d.png`. `MapTool info` reads it back as 1024 × 1024 DXT1.
