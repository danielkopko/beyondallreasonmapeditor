**Red Dust Open Cut 1.0** for [Beyond All Reason](https://www.beyondallreason.info/): a 24 × 24 map for 2v2.

An outback open-cut mine, left only days before the war. The crews walked off mid-shift: the trucks stand where they parked, an ore train waits half-loaded at the loadout, the lighting plants are still lit, and red dust has settled on everything.

- **The pit** runs from the middle of the map to its south edge: eight benches down through red oxide, white kaolin and ochre and purple clays to grey-green fresh rock.
  - **Its shape:** an oval at its north end narrows at a waist, then opens into a broad lobe. The lobe's walls waver, and it is cut back where it meets the edge.
  - **A green pool** lies against the map's edge in its lowest corner.
  - **There is no way round its south end.** The sides meet across the north lane, or down in the pit.
  - **Four haul ramps** run down to the floor, two for each team.
  - **A slide** on the north-east wall is a collapsed slope that bots can climb and vehicles can't.
  - **A bucket wheel excavator** stands on its terrace at the north end, its wheel hanging over the benches below.
  - **Two more bucket wheel excavators** work the lobe's walls, each in a round bite its wheel has cut, standing just taller than the pit is deep, with open floor between them.
  - **On the floor,** an excavator is still at the face with its trucks, a second is idle, and two drill rigs stand on a fresh drill pattern. An excavator works the lobe's east face. Six rich metal spots and a geothermal vent are shared by both teams.
- **The railway** crosses the north lane, from the north edge to a loco shed by the pit's rim.
  - **The train:** three locos and 28 ore wagons, some loaded, some empty and some on a passing siding. It is indestructible, and it walls the lane off.
  - **Three ways through:** past the locos at the map's edge, over the level crossing on the haul road, and between the shed and the rim. Shots and pushes in the north have to find them.
  - **The ore's way to it:** a crusher at the ROM pad feeds an overland conveyor on trestles, past a transfer tower, up into the train loadout. Units can drive under the conveyor.
- **Not symmetric:**
  - **The west** is the plant side: the ROM pad with its ore stockpiles and crusher, the plant's offices, tanks, water tower, generators and comms tower, and the tailings dam with its dried-out decant, ringed with salt.
  - **The east** is the dump side: two terraced waste dumps, paddock-dumped in rows of truck loads, and the workshop with its shed, go-line and fuel farm.
  - The map was checked for fair shares: each team's driving distance to the pit floor, to its own ramps' metal and to the enemy matches within 10% (most within 4%), and both drive the same distance to the level crossing. Each side's metal and reclaimable machines match exactly.
- **Everything was modelled and painted for this map:**
  - **The big machines:** three bucket wheel excavators, 16 haul trucks (empty, loaded, tipping) and 4 excavators.
  - **The railway:** the locos, the wagons, the loco shed, the loadout, the crusher and the conveyor.
  - **Tanks:** diesel and water tanks, large and small, and a water tower.
  - **Smaller machines:** water carts, graders, dozers, wheel loaders, drill rigs, service trucks and crew buses.
  - **Around the site:** shipping containers, windmills with their troughs, a power line, generator sets, stacks of worn-out tyres, an explosives magazine in its bund, drums and pipe.
  - **Reclaim:** 3,370 metal of machines on each side, and 4,200 more in the pit.
- **The outback:** red pindan rolling in low swells and small mounds that slow whatever crosses them, dry washes of pale sand and pebbles, spinifex and saltbush, ghost gums along the creeks and washes, termite mounds, dead trees, ironstone boulders, and breakaways with dark caps and white scarps. The roads stay flat across it.
- **Metal:**
  - four spots at each start (2.0 each);
  - two forward of each team (2.6);
  - one halfway down each of a team's two ramps (2.8);
  - six on the pit floor (3.4).
- Wind is 6 to 18, with no tidal. Teams 0 and 1 start in the west, 2 and 3 in the east.
- The minimap and lobby picture are the map seen straight down in 3D, machines, train and heights included.
- The detail textures and gadgets are from All That Simmers by Nikuksis.

**Install:** put `red_dust_open_cut_1.0.sd7` in BAR's `maps` folder (usually `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Beyond-All-Reason\data\maps`). To see its picture on the lobby's setup screen, put `Red_Dust_Open_Cut_1.0.jpg` in `data\LuaMenu\Images\Minimaps` and restart the lobby.

**Not yet tried in a game.** The reclaim amounts are a first guess, and the map has no start boxes.
